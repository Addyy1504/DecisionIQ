# DecisionIQ

## Problem Statement

Modern businesses generate large amounts of data across sales,
customers, products, marketing and operations.

However, business managers often depend on analysts and dashboards
to understand why KPIs change.

DecisionIQ is an AI-powered decision intelligence platform that allows
business users to analyse company performance using natural language.

The system will combine SQL, analytics, machine learning and generative
AI to identify trends, investigate KPI changes and recommend business
actions.

## Core Business Questions

1. How are revenue and profit changing over time?
2. Which products and categories are most profitable?
3. Which customer segments generate the most value?
4. Which marketing channels produce the highest ROI?
5. Are discounts improving sales or damaging profitability?
6. What factors contribute to product returns?
7. Where are customers dropping out of the purchase funnel?
8. Which unusual changes or anomalies require attention?
9. What is expected revenue for the upcoming period?
10. What happens to profit if pricing or discounts change?
